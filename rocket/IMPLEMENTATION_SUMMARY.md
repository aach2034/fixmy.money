## SEO Implementation Details

### Metadata Standards Applied to All Pages

✅ **Title Tags:** 30-60 characters, keyword-focused, brand included
✅ **Meta Descriptions:** 140-160 characters, action-oriented
✅ **Canonical URLs:** All pages have canonical tags pointing to https://fixmy.money/[route]
✅ **Open Graph Tags:** All pages include OG title, description, URL, type
✅ **H1 Tags:** Exactly one per page, keyword-rich, descriptive
✅ **H2 Tags:** Proper hierarchy, section-based
✅ **Internal Linking:** Every page links to 3-4 relevant pages
✅ **Disclaimers:** All pages include compliance disclaimers
✅ **No Fake Data:** No fabricated statistics, testimonials, or guarantees

### B2B Positioning

✅ **Consistent Messaging:** All pages position FixMy.Money as B2B software for professionals
✅ **No Consumer Positioning:** No language suggesting consumer credit repair services
✅ **No Guaranteed Outcomes:** All pages include disclaimers about credit score improvements
✅ **Compliance Focus:** CROA, FCRA, TSR compliance messaging throughout
✅ **Professional Audience:** Content targets agencies, coaches, consultants, educators

### Compliance & Legal

✅ **Disclaimer Template:** Consistent disclaimer on all pages:
  - "FixMy.Money provides business software for credit repair professionals."
  - "We do not provide consumer credit repair services or guarantee credit outcomes."
  - "Users are responsible for compliance with CROA, FCRA, TSR, and applicable laws."

✅ **No Legal Advice:** No pages provide legal guidance; all recommend consulting attorneys
✅ **CROA Awareness:** Dedicated CROA compliance page and checklist
✅ **Educational Content:** All guides and tools marked as educational
